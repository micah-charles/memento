# M04 evidence — Cantonese and mixed-language validation framework

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Status:** PARTIAL/BLOCKED pending real-provider corpus

## Scope

Measure language behaviour instead of assuming that a generic multilingual label implies Hong Kong Cantonese quality.

## Implementation

`LanguageValidationHarness` evaluates synthetic cases across Cantonese, Mandarin, code-switching, names, places, dates, numbers, hesitations, repetitions, incomplete sentences, uncertainty, and English product names. It reports transcript similarity, separate entity accuracy, code-switch and uncertainty preservation, latency, a disposition, and a machine-readable correction-required flag/count.

## Verification

`dotnet test tests/Memento.Core.Tests/Memento.Core.Tests.csproj --configuration Release` passed **18/18** at the M04 checkpoint. Fixtures are synthetic and deliberately contain no family recordings, private transcripts, or provider credentials.

## Gate decision

**PARTIAL/BLOCKED.** The harness is reproducible and ready for a consented corpus, but no real provider or Hong Kong Cantonese sample was run. No language-quality PASS is claimed.

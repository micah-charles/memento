# M05 evidence — clarification and correction provenance

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Status:** PARTIAL/BLOCKED pending participant UX verification

## Scope

Make uncertainty visible, ask targeted questions for high-value ambiguity, and preserve every correction without rewriting original Source evidence.

## Implementation

- `ClarificationPolicy` weighs entity importance, ambiguity, confidence, and future-memory impact. It avoids asking about low-impact filler while escalating names, places, relationships, dates, identities, preferences, and major events.
- `ClarificationProtocol` stores an initial transcript revision, clarification question and participant response, an optional corrected revision linked to its parent, an explicit outcome, and a speaker-confirmed vocabulary entry when appropriate.
- The protocol supports refusal, “唔記得”, two possibilities, and correction of a previous correction. A later vocabulary entry never changes the original revision or audio Source.
